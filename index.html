The Clinical Blind Spot

Standard Lab Range vs. Optimal Target

Why millions of patients feel fatigued or foggy even when told “everything looks normal.”

Ferritin Iron Stores & Energy
Lab: 15 – 150 ng/mL Optimal: 50 – 120 ng/mL

Levels between 16–35 ng/mL are flagged “normal” even though low iron stores commonly cause low stamina, brain fog, and hair thinning.

Vitamin D (25-OH) Immune & Mood Support
Lab: 30 – 100 ng/mL Optimal: 50 – 80 ng/mL

30 ng/mL merely prevents bone softening; longevity clinicians target 50–80 ng/mL for resilient immunity, mood, and hormone balance.

Fasting Insulin Metabolic Health
Lab: 2.6 – 24.9 µIU/mL Optimal: 2.0 – 6.0 µIU/mL

Elevated fasting insulin signals early insulin resistance years before fasting glucose or HbA1c ever leave the normal range.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TrueRange Labs, data privacy, and reporting.

How is “Optimal Range” different from my lab’s standard range? +

Standard hospital and commercial lab ranges are calculated from population averages—meaning they flag acute pathology rather than optimal vitality. TrueRange Labs maps each marker against tighter preventative and functional longevity ranges used by preventative medicine clinicians.

How long does a lab PDF or photo upload take to analyze? +

Most single-page photos and multi-page clinical PDFs take 15–45 seconds. During this window, our multimodal engine reads every page, normalizes units, cross-checks both standard and functional ranges, and writes tailored clinician follow-up notes.

Which labs and file formats are supported? +

You can upload downloaded PDFs (up to 3.2 MB) or clear smartphone photos (JPG, PNG, HEIC—automatically optimized on upload) from Quest Diagnostics, LabCorp, LifeLabs, Dynacare, hospital patient portals, or international labs.

Is my lab report private and secure? Can I save my results? +

Yes. All uploads travel over encrypted HTTPS/TLS, are processed in-memory to extract biomarker values, and are never sold or shared. You can tap Save / Print PDF right on your results card to export a clean copy for your records or doctor visit.
