Standard Lab Range vs. Optimal Target

Why you can feel exhausted or foggy even when your doctor says “everything looks normal.”

Ferritin Iron Stores & Energy
Lab: 15 – 150 ng/mL Optimal: 50 – 120 ng/mL

Levels between 16–35 ng/mL are flagged “normal” even though low iron stores commonly cause low stamina, brain fog, and hair thinning.

Vitamin D (25-OH) Immune & Mood Support
Lab: 30 – 100 ng/mL Optimal: 50 – 80 ng/mL

30 ng/mL merely prevents bone softening; longevity clinicians target 50–80 ng/mL for resilient immunity, mood, and hormone balance.

Fasting Insulin Metabolic Health
Lab: 2.6 – 24.9 µIU/mL Optimal: 2.0 – 6.0 µIU/mL

Elevated fasting insulin signals early insulin resistance years before fasting glucose or HbA1c ever leave the normal range.
